Contribution Calculation Formula

Formula:
Final Contribution Score = Weight % × (Average Review Score / 100) × 100

Example: If a student has 30% weight and average score of 80 → 30 × (80/100) × 100 = 2400 → normalized to 24.0 out of 100

Simplified: Final Score = Weight % × Average Score (capped at 100)
